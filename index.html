YOUR BODY CAN REACT BEFORE YOU REALIZE IT

Take a Beat doesn’t decide how you feel.
It asks.

Changes in heart rate, movement, available HRV readings and your personal baseline can be a useful signal that something is happening. When Take a Beat notices a meaningful change, it gives you a discreet opportunity to check in.

Notice→Check in→Add context→Reset→Learn

A MOMENT TO CHECK IN

Your watch notices a change.
You provide the context.

You can say you’re okay, a little elevated, very elevated, or exercising. If something was missed, tell Take a Beat. Your feedback is part of how the experience becomes more personal.

Learn what the signals mean—and what they don’t.

The in-app guide explains monitoring, baseline learning, alerts, physical and emotional context, exercise and recovery, breathing, reflection, history, and missed events.

BEFORE YOU JOIN

What to expect from Beta 1

Availability

We’re preparing iPhone and Apple Watch, Android phone, Galaxy Watch, and other Wear OS versions. Invitations depend on supported devices and testing readiness. Apple monitoring guidance and Samsung-specific sensor approval are still pending. Physical testing on non-Samsung Wear OS watches is still needed.

Monitoring & battery

Start monitoring deliberately and check its status on your watch. Apple Watch Active Monitoring uses more battery. Apple Watch Battery Saver relies on readings watchOS makes available, so updates and alerts can be delayed. Modes and sensor availability vary by platform; they do not provide equal alert coverage.

Your role as a tester

Try check-ins, Check Me Now, regulation tools, history, and monitoring in everyday use. Tell us about missed or unnecessary alerts, battery use, confusing screens, and connection problems. We’ll provide installation and feedback instructions with your invitation. Don’t deliberately cause distress to test the app.

Know the limits

Heart rate, movement, and optional supporting signals help identify changes relative to your baseline. HRV and temperature are not available on every watch. The app may miss changes or alert unnecessarily. You supply the physical or emotional context. It does not diagnose anxiety, anger, emotional states, or medical conditions, and is not treatment or emergency monitoring.
