1. Introduction

Take A Beat LLC ("Take a Beat," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and recognizes that physiological and emotional wellbeing information is sensitive.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, collected, used, shared, retained, and deleted when you use the Take a Beat mobile application, smartwatch companion applications, website, and beta testing services (collectively, the "Services").

Take a Beat helps adults recognize physiological changes, reflect on their experiences, and use self-regulation strategies.

Take a Beat is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency response service.

Our Services are intended for adults aged 18 or older.

2. Information Processed by the Application

Depending on your device, enabled features, and permissions, Take a Beat may access or process:

Physiological and activity information

  • Heart rate and changes in heart rate
  • Heart-rate variability (HRV), when available
  • Movement and activity information
  • Supported sleep-related measurements
  • Other available smartwatch sensor measurements used to evaluate physiological changes

Information you provide

  • Emotional check-ins and self-reported experiences
  • Notes, reflections, and journal entries
  • Caffeine intake information
  • Coping strategies and personal preferences
  • Feedback and voluntarily generated reports

Technical information

  • Application version
  • Device and operating system information
  • Smartwatch connection status
  • Sensor availability and monitoring status
  • Error and diagnostic information processed locally or voluntarily included in a report

The specific information available depends on your device, operating system, and enabled features.

Physiological measurements and personal monitoring information are processed locally on your devices.

Take a Beat does not automatically upload this information to our servers or cloud storage.

3. How We Use Information

Take a Beat processes information to:

  1. Establish and adjust personalized physiological baselines.
  2. Identify physiological changes that may warrant a check-in.
  3. Deliver notifications and smartwatch alerts.
  4. Display monitoring history and personal trends.
  5. Provide breathing, grounding, and other self-regulation tools.
  6. Generate user-requested summaries and reports.
  7. Maintain application functionality and support troubleshooting.
  8. Evaluate voluntarily submitted beta feedback to improve application reliability.

Physiological measurements cannot reliably identify a person's emotions or diagnose a medical or psychological condition.

4. Device Permissions

Take a Beat may request access to health measurements, activity sensors, Bluetooth or connected-device functionality, and notifications, depending on the platform.

These permissions support the features you choose to use.

You can manage or revoke permissions through your device's operating system settings.

Revoking certain permissions may limit monitoring, alerts, or other functionality.

5. Local Storage and Security

Physiological information, personal entries, monitoring history, and personalized settings are stored and processed locally on your devices.

Take A Beat LLC does not operate a cloud database that automatically collects this information.

The application does not integrate Firebase, Crashlytics, or other third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

We take reasonable measures to protect information within the Services. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Information may leave your device when you deliberately share a report, submit feedback, or contact us.

Device operating systems and backup services may independently process or retain information according to their own settings and policies.

6. Beta Testing and Daily Reports

During beta testing, participants may generate and manually email reports to Take A Beat LLC.

Reports may contain physiological measurements, monitoring summaries, technical details, and information voluntarily included by the user.

Sending a report requires user action. Reports are not automatically emailed to Take A Beat LLC.

Although reports may use a beta identifier rather than a participant's name, the sender's email address may identify the participant.

Beta reports received by Take A Beat LLC are retained for six months and then deleted, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.

We use submitted reports to investigate issues, evaluate performance, and improve the application.

Email providers may independently retain copies according to their own policies.

7. Therapist and Third-Party Sharing

Take a Beat may help users prepare reports for therapists, healthcare professionals, or other recipients.

Take a Beat does not automatically send these reports.

Users must manually initiate sending through their chosen email application.

Once shared, the recipient and email provider may independently process or retain the information.

8. Analytics and Advertising

Take a Beat does not integrate third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

We do not sell personal information or health information.

We do not use physiological measurements, emotional check-ins, or personal monitoring history for targeted advertising.

9. Website and Beta Registration

Visitors may voluntarily submit contact information through the Take a Beat website to request beta access or receive related communications.

Registration submissions are processed using Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service.

We use submitted information to manage beta participation, provide installation instructions, communicate testing updates, and respond to inquiries.

Web3Forms processes submitted information according to its own privacy practices.

We do not use physiological measurements to target individuals for marketing.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally in Take a Beat generally remains on the user's device until application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled, subject to operating-system storage and backup behavior.

Take a Beat does not currently provide a dedicated in-app data deletion feature.

Beta reports received by Take A Beat LLC are retained for six months.

You may request earlier deletion of reports, beta registration information, or other personal information held by Take A Beat LLC by emailing:

takeabeatapp@gmail.com

Subject: Privacy / Data Deletion Request

We will review and process verified requests in accordance with applicable law.

Deleting the application does not automatically delete reports previously emailed to us or information independently retained by email providers or other third parties.

11. Children's Privacy

Take a Beat is intended for adults aged 18 or older.

We do not knowingly solicit personal or health information from children through the beta program.

If we learn that personal information was collected from an ineligible minor, we will take appropriate steps to delete information under our control, subject to applicable law.

12. Health and Safety Disclaimer

Take a Beat is a wellbeing and self-regulation tool, not an emergency response service or a substitute for professional healthcare.

It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical or mental health condition.

Monitoring and alerts may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate because of sensor limitations, connectivity, operating-system restrictions, or other factors.

An absence of alerts does not indicate that a person is safe, calm, or free from medical or psychological concerns.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

13. Third-Party Platforms and Services

Take a Beat operates on devices and platforms provided by companies such as Apple and Google.

These platforms may independently process information associated with application installation, device operation, permissions, diagnostics, and beta distribution under their respective privacy policies.

The Take a Beat website uses Web3Forms for beta registration.

Voluntarily emailed reports are processed by the sender's and recipient's email providers.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Take a Beat develops, including when new features, integrations, subscriptions, or data-processing practices are introduced.

Updated versions will be published with a revised effective or last-updated date.

Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before materially changing how sensitive information is processed.
